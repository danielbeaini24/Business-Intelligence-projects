# Commercial Pricing Analytics — SQL Server


## Dataset

**E-Commerce Dataset** available on Kaggle.

### Phase 1 — Revenue & Customer Performance

Analyzed:

* Total revenue
* Revenue trends
* Top customers
* Country performance
* Product and category revenue
* Customer revenue concentration
* Average transaction value

### Phase 2 — Profitability & Pricing Analysis

Analyzed:

* Total cost and gross profit
* Gross margin
* Customer profitability
* Loss-making customers
* Product profitability
* Category profitability
* Selling price versus product cost
* High-volume, low-margin products

### Phase 3 — Commercial Strategy & Advanced Analytics

Analyzed:

* Monthly revenue trends
* Month-over-month revenue changes
* Customer revenue rankings
* Revenue concentration
* High-value, low-margin customers
* Pricing-review candidates
* Commercial review priorities
* Customer portfolio segmentation

## Key Business Insights

* **Revenue:** 60,398 transactions generated approximately **$29.36M** in total revenue.
* **Transaction Value:** Average transaction value was approximately **$486**.
* **Customer Concentration:** The Top 10 customers represented approximately **0.45%** of total revenue, indicating a highly diversified customer base.
* **Profitability:** Gross profit was approximately **$11.69M**, representing an overall gross margin of **39.8%**.
* **Pricing Opportunities:** No customers fell below the illustrative **20% gross-margin threshold** used for the pricing-review analysis.
